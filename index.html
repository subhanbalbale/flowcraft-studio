Getting you to the top of the page

Someone nearby searches for what you sell, or asks ChatGPT or Gemini who to call. Five things decide whether your business is the answer.

Custom sites built to rank

A site written to be found, not retrofitted afterwards. Clean markup, fast mobile loads, proper headings and internal linking, and schema for the things your business actually is.

SEO and AEO

Classic search optimisation for the ranked results, and answer engine optimisation so a featured snippet or a People Also Ask box quotes your page instead of a competitor's.

GEO and LLM optimisation

Customers now ask ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity and Google's AI Overviews who to call, and those answers are assembled from sources a model can parse and trust. That means your business facts matching everywhere you appear, entity schema a model reads without guessing, pages written to answer one question in a quotable paragraph, and an llms.txt telling AI crawlers what you actually do.

Search Console and GA4

Verified Search Console, submitted sitemaps, indexing and coverage problems fixed at the source, and GA4 events on the things worth money: calls, direction taps and WhatsApp clicks.

Google Maps profile

Categories, services and attributes set properly, address verified, photos geotagged, posts and Q&A kept active, and citations cleaned up across directories. That is the work that moves a listing toward the three results people actually call.

Common questions

Who can build me a website in Navi Mumbai?

Subhan Balbale, an independent full-stack engineer based in Navi Mumbai, builds them himself. A ready-made SiteMaker site is ₹5,999 once and live in 24 hours. A custom build is quoted on the work and takes three to seven days. You message him directly on WhatsApp, not an account manager.

How does the Smart Automated Review System work?

A customer is asked for a review at the moment it is easiest to give one: an automated WhatsApp message after their visit, or a QR code at the counter. They tap a rating, pick the parts of the visit worth mentioning, and the system writes the review for them to edit and post to Google in one tap. Anyone with a problem gets a direct line to the manager in the same flow.

Are there any monthly hosting fees for SiteMaker websites?

No. SiteMaker sites run on serverless hosting that is included, so there is no monthly bill and nothing to renew. You pay ₹5,999 once. A domain of your own is the only separate cost, around ₹800 to ₹1,500 a year paid to the registrar in your name.

How quickly can my website be delivered and live?

Within 24 hours of payment. You can build and preview the whole thing free before that on mysitemaker.in, so you only pay once you have seen the finished site.

Can I use my own domain name, like mybusiness.in?

Yes. Any .in, .com, .store or .co domain connects to your SiteMaker site, with an SSL certificate included at no extra cost. It is registered in your name and connected for free.

How do you help local businesses rank on Google Maps?

We set your Google Business Profile categories and services properly, verify the address, geotag the photos, keep posts and Q&A active, and clean up citations across directories. Paired with a steady flow of genuine reviews, that is what moves a listing toward the Google Maps local 3-Pack.

Do you build billing software for trades other than the five listed?

Usually yes. Pharmacy, GST, transport, gym and restaurant are the ones already built, so they start furthest along, but most counter-side businesses share the same bones: items, parties, invoices, stock and a daybook.
