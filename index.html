Common questions.

How does the Smart Automated Review System work?

The system triggers automated review invitations over WhatsApp, SMS, or checkout QR codes after a customer interaction. Positive ratings (4–5 stars) open your Google review box with 5 stars pre-selected. Unhappy ratings (1–3 stars) route to a private WhatsApp form so you resolve disputes directly before public reviews are posted.

Are there any monthly hosting fees for SiteMaker websites?

No. SiteMaker websites are built on serverless architecture, so there is no monthly hosting bill. You pay ₹5,999 once and own the code and the domain.

How quickly can my website be delivered and live?

Usually 24 to 48 hours once your Google Maps listing and business details are confirmed. You can test and preview your site instantly on mysitemaker.in.

Can I use my own domain name, like mybusiness.in?

Yes. Any .in, .com, .store, or .co domain connects to your SiteMaker site, with an SSL security certificate included at no extra cost.

How do you help local businesses rank on Google Maps?

We optimize your Google Business Profile categories, verify your address, geotag photos, and structure your service catalog. Paired with regular 5-star Google reviews from our automated review system, this moves your listing into the Google Maps local 3-Pack.
