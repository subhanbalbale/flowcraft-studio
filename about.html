01

Why this exists

“If it doesn’t bring you customers,
it’s decoration.”

A shop owner in Navi Mumbai can pay ₹30,000 for a website that takes two months, loads slowly and never rings their phone. I kept meeting people in that position and could not explain to them why it had to cost that much, so I started building the other version.

A website is not the goal. A phone call is. So I start from the thing that actually brings you work, build only what serves it, and ship it in days. You get something you can edit yourself afterwards, and you own all of it.

Three to seven days · you talk to the person writing the code · one price, no retainer

02

What I do

  1. Websites that look expensive

    A page that looks like a brand charging properly, not a template somebody filled in. It also has to open instantly on mobile data outside your shop, because four seconds sends that customer back to Maps.

    Clean code · sub-second loads · mobile first

  2. AI that works the night shift

    The repeated parts of running a shop are what software is actually good at. Pulling prospects off Google Maps, answering the same five WhatsApp questions at eleven at night, building a campaign from a list you already have.

    Gemini API · autonomous agents · tool calling

  3. Apps of my own

    SiteMaker is a self-serve website app an owner uses to build their own site in minutes. LeadsFlowPro finds local businesses that still have no website. StarScanner is in build. Products I run and support myself, not client work with my name taken off.

    SiteMaker · LeadsFlowPro · StarScanner

  4. Software that runs the counter

    Pharmacy, GST, transport, gym and restaurant billing built around how the shop floor actually works, with the ground Marg, Tally, Vyapar and Petpooja cover. Bought once, not licensed every year.

    Billing · stock · GST returns · daybook

  5. Reviews that keep arriving

    Happy customers rarely write a review because a blank box is work. A WhatsApp message or a counter QR asks at the right moment and drafts the wording for them. Every customer gets the same public Google link, with no gating.

    WhatsApp triggers · drafted wording · SiteMaker auto-sync

LeadsFlowPro

Finds local businesses with no website

Give it a pin code and a trade and it reads the public Google Maps listings for that area, then filters to the businesses with no website of their own. Those owners are already being found on Maps with nowhere to send anyone. You work down the list and open each WhatsApp thread yourself. It sends nothing on its own.
