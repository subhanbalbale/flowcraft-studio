Why does FlowCraft exist?

Because a shop owner in Mumbai can pay ₹30,000 for a website that takes two months, loads slowly, and never rings their phone. I kept meeting people in that position and could not explain to them why it had to cost that much, so I started building the other version.

"If it doesn't bring you customers, it's decoration."

A website is not the goal — a phone call is. So I start from the thing that actually brings you work, build only what serves it, and ship it in days. You get something you can edit yourself afterwards, and you own all of it.
