Are there any monthly hosting fees for SiteMaker websites?

No. SiteMaker websites are built on serverless hosting, so there is no monthly bill. You pay ₹5,999 once and own the code and the domain.

How quickly can my website be delivered and live?

Usually 24 to 48 hours once I have your Google Maps listing and your content. Because you work with me directly, there are no agency meetings in the way.

Can I use my own domain, like mybusiness.in?

Yes. Any .in, .com, .store or .co domain connects to your SiteMaker site, with an SSL certificate included at no extra cost.

How do you help local businesses rank on Google Maps?
