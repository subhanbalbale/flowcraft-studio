Frequently Asked Questions

Everything You Need to Know

Common questions about NFC compatibility, order customization, and delivery across India.

How do Smart NFC business cards and standees work? ↓

Each card, stand, or keychain contains an integrated rewritable NFC microchip. When an iPhone or Android phone is tapped against it, the phone's built-in sensor instantly launches your digital card, Google review page, or website. No batteries or charging are required.

Do clients or customers need to install an app? ↓

No. Modern iPhones (iPhone XS and newer) and all NFC-enabled Android devices have native tap detection built in. Tapping immediately displays a notification on the phone's screen with zero apps required.

How does the Google Review NFC Tap Standee help local businesses? ↓

Customers often forget to leave reviews once they leave your store or clinic. Placing an acrylic tap standee on your counter allows customers to tap their phone while billing and open your 5-star review box in two seconds.

Can I change my phone number or review link after receiving my NFC product? ↓

Yes. FlowCraft NFC products connect to dynamic destinations, meaning you can update your phone number, social media links, catalog, or review URL anytime without needing to replace the physical hardware.

Do you deliver custom NFC products across India? ↓

Yes. We program, test, and ship custom NFC cards, review standees, and keychains directly from Mumbai to anywhere in India via tracked courier services.
