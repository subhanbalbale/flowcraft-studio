Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

How does the 4–5 star Google redirect work? +

When a customer taps 4 or 5 stars on your review landing page or WhatsApp message, they are immediately redirected to your official Google Maps business review modal with 5 stars already selected. All they have to do is tap "Post".

What happens if an unhappy customer gives 1, 2, or 3 stars? +

The Sentiment Shield triggers immediately: instead of directing them to Google Maps, it opens a prefilled WhatsApp conversation with the business owner or clinic manager saying: "I noticed your recent visit wasn't ideal. Please tell us what went wrong so we can resolve this right away." This allows you to fix complaints privately before they hurt your public rating.

Is this compliant with Google Review guidelines? +

Yes. We do not generate artificial bot reviews or block customers from searching Google independently. The system provides a convenient direct customer service routing channel for unhappy customers to resolve issues with management.

Do customers have to install an app? +

No. The entire system operates in mobile browsers and through regular WhatsApp. It requires zero installations, logins, or passwords for your customers.

How fast can you set this up for my business? +

Setup typically takes 24 to 48 hours once you provide your Google Business Profile URL and your manager's WhatsApp number.
