Common questions

How does the 4 to 5 star Google redirect work?

When a customer taps 4 or 5 stars on your review landing page or WhatsApp message, they go straight to your official Google Maps review dialog with 5 stars already selected. All they do is tap "Post".

What happens if an unhappy customer gives 1, 2 or 3 stars?

They get the same Google review link as everyone else, and next to it a direct WhatsApp line to you: "Sorry that was not right. Tell us what went wrong and we will sort it." Nothing is hidden and no rating is filtered. You simply hear about the problem while the customer is still in front of you instead of reading about it a week later.

Is this compliant with Google review guidelines?

Yes. Review gating is a way of filtering customers by their star rating before deciding whether to show them a public review link, and it is what Google's review policy and the FTC's review rule prohibit. This system does not do it. Everyone gets the same public link, nothing is offered in exchange for a review, and no review is written by a bot.

Do customers have to install an app?

No. The whole system runs in mobile browsers and in regular WhatsApp. Your customers install nothing and log in to nothing.

How fast can you set this up for my business?

Usually 24 to 48 hours, once you provide your Google Business Profile URL and your manager's WhatsApp number.
