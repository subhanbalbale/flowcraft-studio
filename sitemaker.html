What ₹5,999 covers

Both columns, so there are no surprises on the call. The second one is the part most quotes leave out until you have already paid.

Included in the fee

  • A complete site built on a design made for your trade, not a blank template you have to fill in.
  • Your photos, hours, address and reviews pulled in from your Google Maps listing.
  • Hosting and an SSL certificate, with no monthly bill attached to either. You do not pay ₹500 or ₹1,000 every month the way you would on Shopify or Wix.
  • Local search schema set up, so Google and AI assistants can read what you do and where.
  • A WhatsApp enquiry button wired to your number on every page.
  • Editing access, so you can change your own prices and text afterwards without calling me.

Not included

  • The domain name. A .in or .com runs roughly ₹700 to ₹1,200 a year, paid to the registrar, in your name. I connect it for free and do not resell it to you at a markup.
  • Photography. The Maps import covers most businesses. If your listing has no good photos, that is a real gap and no website fixes it.
  • Online payments or a cart. SiteMaker is built to produce enquiries and bookings. If you need to sell products online, say so up front, because that is a different build.
  • Ongoing ad campaigns. Not something I run. The site and your Google profile are built to earn traffic without paying for it.

Questions owners ask

Is ₹5,999 really the whole cost?

For the site, yes. One payment, no monthly hosting bill afterwards. The only other cost is the domain name, around ₹700 to ₹1,200 a year paid directly to the registrar in your own name.

How can it be this much cheaper than an agency?

Two reasons, and neither is a catch. The designs already exist. I built sixty of them across twenty trades, so your site starts finished instead of from a blank page. And there is no agency around me: no account manager, no sales team, no office. A ₹30,000 quote is mostly paying for those.

What happens if I want changes later?

Text and prices you change yourself from the editor, and that access comes with the site. For a structural change, like adding a whole new section, message me and I will quote it. There is no retainer either way.

What do you need from me to start?

A link to your Google Maps listing, your WhatsApp number, and your price list if you want one on the site. That is usually enough. If you have no Maps listing yet, we set that up first, because it matters more than the website.

What if I already have a website?

Send me the link before you pay anything. If it loads fast and brings you calls, you do not need a new one and I will say so. Most of the sites I get sent are slow, were built years ago, and are costing their owner a monthly fee for the privilege.

Who owns the site once it is paid for?

You do, the code and the domain both. If you ever want to move it elsewhere or hand it to another developer, you can, and I will send the files over. Nothing is held hostage to keep you paying.
